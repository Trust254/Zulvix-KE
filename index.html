About Zulvix-KE

Zulvix-KE is a premier digital asset tap & earn platform originally established and published in 2015. We provide secure, automated asset valuation loops and micro-task earnings for active digital participants across the region.

  • Cooldown Compliance: Users must respect the 12-hour cooling timer after completing daily asset taps.
  • Deposit Verification: All activation and top-up deposits must match exact amounts with verified M-Pesa tracking numbers.
