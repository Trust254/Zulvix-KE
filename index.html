About Zulvix-KE & Platform Policies

Zulvix-KE is the name used by this online platform for account access, tier information, wallet records and customer support. These pages explain how the platform currently operates. They do not represent a claim that Zulvix-KE is a bank, investment firm, government programme, or licensed financial institution.

About Us

The platform provides registered users with an online dashboard to view their account, available tiers, activity records, payment requests and support messages.

Users should rely on the information displayed in their own account and on official platform notices. The operator's registered legal name, registration details and business address should be independently verified before making payments.

Terms & Conditions

Keep your login details private and provide accurate account and payment information. Do not impersonate another person, submit false payment references, exploit system errors or interfere with another user's account.

Requests and transactions may be checked before approval. A submitted request is not proof of payment, and an approved withdrawal is not marked paid until the payment has actually been sent and confirmed.

Tier fees, deposits and other charges must be reviewed before submission. No earnings, rewards, investment return or withdrawal timing is guaranteed. Do not pay money you cannot afford to lose, and seek independent advice if you are unsure.

Earning & Level Policy

Level 1 is free for its first 10 days. Beginning on day 11, each tap displays a warning that Level 1 will lock within the following 24 hours unless the user upgrades. Taps remain available during that warning period. Once the 24-hour warning period ends, Level 1 tapping and earning are locked until the user upgrades.

The timer must be based on a verified Level 1 activation date. Existing accounts without a verified activation date are not to be assigned an assumed start date or retroactively locked.

Displayed rewards are subject to the platform's validation rules and are not a promise of guaranteed income or investment returns. Review all tier costs and conditions before paying.

Withdrawal Policy

Withdrawal processing is scheduled Monday to Friday, 08:00–16:00 East Africa Time (Kenya time). No withdrawal payments are processed on weekends. Requests received outside these hours remain queued for the next working window.

Requests are subject to account and balance checks and administrator review. “Approved” means authorised for payment; it does not mean the M-Pesa payment has been sent. The status changes to “Paid” only after the administrator confirms the actual payment.

Make sure the destination phone number is correct. If a request is rejected, the platform should show the reason and restore any amount reserved for that request.

Privacy Policy

The platform may process your name, phone number, login/account details, account activity, wallet transactions, payment references, withdrawal destination and support messages to operate accounts, verify requests, prevent abuse and respond to support issues.

Do not submit passwords, PINs or one-time verification codes in support messages. Access to personal information should be limited to authorised platform administration and relevant service providers, and disclosures should only occur where necessary to operate the service or comply with law.

For a privacy request or account concern, use the Support section in your account. The platform operator should publish a verified privacy contact and retention schedule before launch.

Account Restriction & Appeals

Accounts may be restricted or blocked for suspected fraud, false payment details, abuse or serious rule violations. Administrators should record the reason and may restore access after review.

Blocking an account does not itself cancel the user's balance or erase transaction history. Users may contact Support to request a review. Inactivity for five days may be flagged for administrator review; it does not automatically confiscate funds or permanently block an account.

Account Appeal / Contact Support

If you cannot access your account, enter your registered phone number and explain your appeal.
